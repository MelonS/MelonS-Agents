How it works

The scaffold is general-purpose. Short-form video is the v1 mission type — chosen because the deliverable is visually verifiable and the failure modes are quick to catch. Two short-form modes are now live: faceless-short (narration-driven, Sonnet script + Kokoro TTS) and music-video (music-as-sole-audio, beat-aligned cuts + glitch micro-edits on detected drum onsets).

Pipeline (faceless-short mission)

  1. Script. scripts/gen-script-claude.sh calls Sonnet with a tight hook + factual-coherence prompt, auto-scores the result, retries with feedback if below threshold.
  2. TTS. Kokoro-ONNX (English) or macOS Yuna (Korean). Apache 2.0 licensed, runs offline.
  3. Captions. whisper.cpp transcribes for timing, then script-aware token alignment fixes proper nouns, then a single-line splitter enforces ≤ 28 chars per cue.
  4. B-roll. Per-window keyword extraction (8 windows) → Pexels Videos API. Each window's clip matches what's being said at that moment.
  5. Render. ffmpeg 9:16 screen-fill, libass burned captions, top-left source attribution overlay.
  6. QA. Mission-level retry on failure, with the previous qa-report prepended on retry attempts.

Three-layer reactive audit

  • L1 — post-commit hook. Drift-risk commits (anything under agents/, .claude/agents/, config/, CLAUDE.md, the operator contract) fire audit-run.sh contract within ~30 s.
  • L2 — 15-min mission-anomaly poll. New blocker files or QA-FAIL bursts trigger a focused audit. No-op (zero tokens) when nothing's wrong.
  • L3 — daily 03:00 baseline. launchd fires the full sweep. Catches anything L1 + L2 missed.

The pattern is Reactor + Hook (files as events), not Observer — subagents in this repo aren't long-running observables.

Cost-routing rule

The architectural lesson from a real failure: applying "Tier 2 (local) = default" to every pipeline stage produces a quality ceiling.

  • Mechanical, high-volume stages (transcribe, render, fetch) — local. Token cost would be ruinous at scale.
  • One-shot creative stages (script hook, factual framing) — Claude. ~500 tokens per call, operationally negligible against the existing subscription quota, and quality compounds over the next 60 seconds of viewing.

Full reasoning in docs/cost-model.md.

music-video mission (post-pivot, 2026-05-17)

Distinct from faceless-short. Music is the sole audio; no narration; no captions. Selected by the operator after viewing eight narration-driven music-trial pilots and pivoting to a format that lets the music carry the short.

  • Beat extraction. aubiotrack finds real beats; sub-beat noise rejected. Cuts land every Nth beat (default 12 — about one cut per 7.5 s at 95 BPM).
  • Variable speed. Per-clip setpts by mood — slow scenes 0.55×, ambient 0.70×, active 0.80×, natural 1.00×. The music drives the visual pace.
  • Glitch micro-edits. 0.2 s reverse + 0.2 s forward jump-cut at detected aubioonset drum hits — but only on clips classified as static-camera, so the frame doesn't shake during the glitch.
  • Vintage lo-fi treatment. Subtle film grain + soft vignette + Gaussian zoom-pulse on each glitch onset. Tunable per render via env vars.

Pick reasoning + format spec: decision-log.md. Music files are local-only by policy (a "use in video" license ≠ "redistribute the file" license — see assets/music/README.md).

Self-evaluated scorecard

Each pilot rated on five retention-mapping axes (Hook, Visual sync, Readability, Factual coherence, Production polish). Scores by Claude, not a viewer panel — they're a structured progress signal until real platform watch-time data replaces them.
