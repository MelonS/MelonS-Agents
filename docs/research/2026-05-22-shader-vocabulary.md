# Shader Vocabulary Survey — 2026-05-22

Companion research for quality-bar directive #4 (2026-05-22): expand
the shader catalog beyond the current 15 effects and document which
shader fits which mood / genre.  Until now genre presets had a single
hard-coded shader choice (e.g., kpop_ballad → halation, synthwave →
beat_burst); the operator's observation is that the resulting renders
read as monotonous across the channel.

This document catalogs shader techniques used in production music
videos, maps them to mood families, and identifies catalog gaps to
close.

## Mood families (operator-stated, validated against current presets)

| Family | Tracks belonging | Visual contract |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| Atmospheric | ambient, drone, classical, dreamcore, shoegaze | Slow, continuous, no cuts.  Warm light or cool void.  Shader is the visual |
| Mellow | lofi_hiphop, jazz, cottagecore | Warm grain, soft light, paper-feel.  Static-camera B-roll.  Shader is comfort |
| Nostalgic | citypop, vaporwave, synthwave | Saturated color, light leaks, retro UI.  Cuts on phrase.  Shader is style |
| Vocal-emotional | kpop_ballad, rnb | Sparse, warm, low-key.  Vocal carries; visuals minimal |
| Vocal-pop | uspop, kpop_dance | Clean, bright, energetic.  Beat-synced |
| Aggressive | phonk, hyperpop, techno | Glitch, strobe, hard-cut.  Shader IS the energy |
| Hi-fi clean | house | Smooth, modern, gradient color.  Shader for shine |

## Current catalog (15 shaders)

### Classic (atmosphere)
- `pond` — animated water-surface displacement (geq + displace).  Suits jazz / lofi / ambient.
- `breathing` — slow 5s-period upscale zoom.  Suits ambient / stillzoom.
- `halation` — warm bloom around bright pixels.  Suits jazz / citypop / kpop_ballad / rnb / lofi.
- `combo` — pond + halation with phrase envelope.  Suits lofi-jazz climax.

### Genre-coded (style accent)
- `scanline` — horizontal scanlines.  Suits synthwave / retro.
- `chromatic_split` — RGB shift.  Suits phonk / shoegaze / vaporwave.
- `neon_edge` — edge-detect neon colorize.  Suits synthwave alt.
- `vhs` — VHS noise + chroma blur.  Suits dreamcore / hauntology.
- `saturation_pulse` — 2Hz sin saturation oscillation.  Suits uspop / house.
- `kaleidoscope` — 4-fold mirror.  Suits psychedelic moments.

### Beat-synced (event accent)
- `beat_burst` — bright pulse at onset.  Suits synthwave / hyperpop.
- `strobe` — brief flash at onset.  Suits techno / hyperpop.
- `shake` — micro camera shake at onset.  Suits phonk / drift.
- `color_burst` — hue swap at onset.  Suits house / kpop_dance.
- `light_rays` — static god rays + scanline darken.  Suits techno / synthwave.

## Identified gaps

Categories with weak or no coverage:

1. **Light leaks / film burns** — Super-8 / Bolex cinematic accent.  No current shader.
   Visual: random colored light bleeds across the frame, position drift, brief duration.
   Mood fit: cottagecore, jazz, citypop, lofi, shoegaze.

2. **Dust specks / film texture** — 8mm celluloid drift.  No current shader.
   Visual: tiny floating particles overlay, slight perspective drift.
   Mood fit: jazz, lofi_hiphop, cottagecore, classical.

3. **Duotone color mapping** — Spotify Wrapped style two-color quantize.  No current shader.
   Visual: image collapsed to 2-tone gradient (e.g., magenta + cyan).
   Mood fit: synthwave (signature look), citypop, uspop pop-art moments.

4. **Trail / echo** — Motion ghosting.  No current shader.
   Visual: previous frames bleed into current at reduced opacity.
   Mood fit: techno, house, hyperpop, drone.

5. **Vignette pulse** — Breathing dark border.  No current shader.
   Visual: vignette radius pulses to BPM.
   Mood fit: kpop_ballad, rnb, citypop, jazz, shoegaze.

6. **Paper / canvas grain** — Distinct from film grain.  No current shader.
   Visual: scanned-paper texture overlay; not flicker-grain.
   Mood fit: cottagecore, jazz, classical, indie acoustic.

7. **Posterize / halftone** — Comic / print style.  No current shader.
   Visual: discrete color steps; halftone dot pattern.
   Mood fit: hyperpop, phonk, vaporwave, pop-art kpop_dance.

8. **Soft-bloom (gentle halation)** — Quieter than `halation`.  No current shader.
   Visual: low-intensity bloom for ballads where full halation is too loud.
   Mood fit: kpop_ballad, rnb, ambient slow.

## ffmpeg implementability notes

Most candidates can ship with ffmpeg's `geq`, `hue`, `blend`, `curves`,
`gblur`, `eq`, `noise`, and `overlay` filters — no GLSL / no extra
binary.  Specific patterns:

- **Light leak**: `[0:v][leak]blend=all_mode=screen:all_opacity=0.4`
  where `[leak]` is generated by `nullsrc → geq` producing color blobs
  whose centers drift over time.

- **Dust speck**: `noise=alls=8:allf=t,blend=all_mode=screen:all_opacity=0.15`
  with a sparse threshold so most of the noise field is black.  Slight
  drift via `crop=...:x='0.5*t'` to give the speck-floating illusion.

- **Duotone**: `geq=lum='lum(X,Y)':cb='128 + lum(X,Y)*0.6':cr='128 - lum(X,Y)*0.4'`
  (the exact cb/cr coefficients map black→color1 and white→color2).
  Cleanest result combines with prior `eq=saturation=0` to neutralize
  the source colors.

- **Trail / echo**: `tmix=frames=4:weights='2 1 1 1'` — weighted
  average of the last N frames produces motion trails.  Cheap.

- **Vignette pulse**: ffmpeg's `vignette` filter supports time-varying
  `angle` parameter, but eval=frame isn't always supported in static
  builds (we already hit this on ffmpeg-static 8.1.1).  Alternative:
  drive via a sine expression in the angle field directly using `t`.

- **Paper grain**: `noise=alls=4:allf=t+u,format=gray,
  blend=all_mode=overlay:all_opacity=0.20`.  Texture variant of grain.

- **Posterize**: `lutyuv=y='if(gt(val,200),255,if(gt(val,128),192,
  if(gt(val,64),128,0)))'` — discrete tone steps.  Halftone version
  adds an alpha-modulated checker pattern.

- **Soft-bloom**: same filter graph as halation but with the `screen`
  blend opacity dropped from 0.55 → 0.25 and the gaussian sigma
  decreased.  Effectively halation × 0.4 intensity.

## Recommended catalog expansion

Sequence ships in stages so each shader can be tested in isolation:

### Stage 1 — broadly-applicable accents (this commit batch)

- `light_leak` — high impact, broadly applicable; fixes the "every
  short looks the same" feel because the leak position is randomized
  per render.
- `duotone` — distinct enough to be a signature variant; pairs with
  citypop, synthwave, vaporwave.
- `vignette_pulse` — gentle and broadly useful; fills the
  "kpop_ballad needs MORE atmosphere but LESS effect" gap.

### Stage 2 — texture (shipped 2026-05-22)

- `paper_grain` — static yuv noise (allf=u, no temporal flicker) at
  low intensity.  Adds organic texture without movement.
- `dust_speck` — sparse noise field thresholded high so most pixels
  are zero; screen-blended over base.  8mm celluloid drift feel.
- `posterize` — lutyuv quantizes luminance to 4 bands; saturation
  boosted to compensate for tone-band flatness.  Comic / pop-art.

### Stage 3 — temporal (shipped 2026-05-22)

- `trail_echo` — `tmix=frames=4:weights='2 1 1 1'` produces motion
  trails from recent frames.  Cheap temporal ghosting.
- `soft_bloom` — halation mechanic with sigma halved (10 vs 22) and
  screen opacity dropped (0.25 vs 0.55).  Quiet glow for ballads.

## Per-genre re-mapping

After Stage 1 ships, preset shader fields may shift toward newer
options.  Suggested re-mappings (operator decides):

| Genre | Current | After Stage 1 |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| kpop_ballad | halation | vignette_pulse OR halation |
| rnb         | halation | vignette_pulse OR halation |
| citypop     | halation | light_leak OR halation |
| synthwave   | beat_burst | duotone (drop) + beat_burst (cuts) |
| vaporwave   | chromatic_split | duotone |
| cottagecore | halation | light_leak |

The shifts move the channel further from "every short looks the
same" — different shaders read distinctly even at small thumbnail
sizes.

## Out of scope (parking for a later phase)

- True GLSL shaders (mpv + libplacebo).  ffmpeg-only constraint
  inherited from the music-video pipeline; rewriting for GLSL would
  break portability of records output.
- AI-driven style transfer (Stable Diffusion runwayML).  Money
  firewall + render-time budget.
- Per-frame procedural noise generators.  Cost too high for 9:16
  vertical render at 1080p×30fps within current pipeline timing.
