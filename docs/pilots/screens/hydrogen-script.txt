What if we told you that hydrogen is the most abundant element in the universe, making up a staggering 75 percent of its mass?

Hydrogen's ubiquity can be attributed to its lightest atomic weight and simplest molecular structure, allowing it to form bonds with other elements in countless ways. In fact, water – H2O – is composed of two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom, highlighting the element's versatility.

But what about our own bodies? Hydrogen makes up a surprising 10 percent of human tissue, playing a crucial role in energy production and cellular function. It's also present in every molecule of fat, protein, and carbohydrate we consume.

In short, hydrogen is the unsung hero of the universe – the invisible foundation upon which all life is built.
