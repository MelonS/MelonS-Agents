What if hydrogen, the lightest and most abundant element in the universe, makes up a mere 10 percent of our bodies?

Hydrogen is the backbone of water, making up about two-thirds of its molecular structure. It's also present in carbohydrates, fats, and proteins, with an average human body consisting of roughly 60% hydrogen by weight.

In fact, if you were to extract all the hydrogen from a single human body, it would weigh around 1 kilogram – roughly the same as a large bag of sugar. Despite its abundance in our bodies, hydrogen is often overlooked and underappreciated.
