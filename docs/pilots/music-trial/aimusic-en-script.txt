An 8-word text prompt now produces a chart-quality, 3-minute vocal track in under 90 seconds.

Suno launched its v3 model in March 2024. Udio followed in April. Both tools work the same way: type a phrase — "upbeat funk with male vocals" — hit generate, and receive a finished song with drums, bass, guitar, and lead vocal, fully mixed and mastered. The file is immediately distributable to Spotify, Apple Music, and TikTok without opening a DAW.

But in June 2024, Universal, Sony, and Warner filed suit against both companies, alleging unlicensed training on copyrighted recordings. The suits are still active. The tools kept shipping.

By 2025, Suno's free mobile tier gives any user 10 songs a day. A union studio session in Los Angeles runs a 3-hour minimum at AFM scale — over $450 in musician fees before a single note is tracked.

A Nashville guitarist is now bidding for jingle work against a text prompt.
